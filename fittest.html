Muscat Fittest started on the gym floor at Core & More, on Level 1 of Panorama Mall in Bowsher, as a judged competition for people who train all year.

The first edition put members and experienced competitors on the same floor, in RX and Scaled divisions. The second edition is on 18 December 2026.

The 1st edition workouts

Four workouts decided the first leaderboard. The 2nd edition workouts will be announced before the event on @coreandmoreco.

3 rounds for time · 12 min cap

Engine test

  1. 01Calorie row or BikeErg24 / 18 cal
  2. 02Dual dumbbell thrusters, 22.5 / 15 kg15 reps
  3. 03Burpee box jump-overs, 24 / 20 in12 reps
Standards
Full hip and knee extension at the top of every thruster. Two-foot take-off on RX box jump-overs; Scaled may step over.
Scoring
Time to finish all 3 rounds. If you hit the cap, every rep left adds 1 second.
Focus
Aerobic capacity and pacing across three rounds.
