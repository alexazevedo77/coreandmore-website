The gym

Five zones,
six coaches.

Core & More is a boxing and fitness gym in Muscat, with a ladies-only zone, a boxing zone, a BoxFit zone and two weights zones.

Meet the coaches Book a first session
Who trains here

Adults, women and kids, in mixed and women-only classes.

Mornings start with mixed boxing at 06:00, followed by mixed and women-only fitness classes until mid-morning.

Kids’ boxing and kickboxing start at 17:00. Evenings run until 21:00 with boxing fitness, kickboxing and mixed fitness.
